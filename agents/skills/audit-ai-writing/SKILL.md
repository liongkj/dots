# Writing Audit

Review the text for audience fit, unnecessary prose, unsupported claims, and broken publication markup. Read [patterns.md](patterns.md) when a specific editorial or citation check needs detail.

Ground findings in exact snippets or locations. Prioritize factual and citation errors, then recurring clarity problems, then local polish. Verify sources when citation support matters; distinguish unavailable evidence from a confirmed error.

Report the issue, its effect on the reader, and a concrete correction. Group repeated symptoms and keep the report proportional to the text. For a rewrite request, deliver the revised text; include explanation only where useful.

Treat style cues as quality concerns, not proof of AI authorship. Preserve the writer's voice and avoid detector-score claims. Edit files only within the requested scope.
